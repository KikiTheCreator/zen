# 05 — Mental (Tilt, Focus, Learning Mindset)

## What the research says
- **Tilt** = anger/frustration that degrades decisions. It originally comes from poker; a
  21-item questionnaire separates **emotional/behavioral tilt** and **cognitive tilt**
  (self-control, risk taking). (Frontiers in Psychology, 2024)
- A **TILT scale** for gamers has been validated, and it links tilt with toxic behavior and
  problematic gaming. (Univ. of Chichester, 2024)
- In LoL players, **exposure to toxic behavior predicted tilt** beyond emotion regulation
  and mental toughness. (APS poster; preliminary)
- Experts describe tilt as different from plain anger, and **propensity to tilt** varies
  between people. (DiGRA Australia 2025)
- LoL performance is a **dynamic system**, not isolated stats, so measure many things
  together. (Sharpe et al., 2025 systematic review)
- Caveat: most studies are small, so present features as habits, not medical claims.

## Practical principles (from coaching + sports psychology)
1. **Process goals, not results**: "this game: CS@10 ≥ 70 and look at the map" instead
   of "win". You control the process, not the teammates.
2. **Stop-loss**: stop after 2 losses in a row, or when you feel angry. Most climbing
   advice agrees.
3. **/mute all** (or mute chat) by default; it reduces toxicity exposure, the main predictor.
4. **Ownership**: in review, only look at *your* mistakes. "What could I have done?"
5. **Short routine** before queue: water, posture, 1 focus goal, breathing (e.g. 4-second
   in / 6-second out × 5).
6. **Sleep/session length**: performance drops in long sessions; cap 3–5 ranked games.

## Features
- **Pre-game check-in** (5 seconds): mood 1–5 + "today's focus" (auto-suggested from last
  review).
- **Session tracker**: detect loss streaks and play time from match history → suggest a break
  (not forced; never a game decision).
- **Post-game reflection** (3 questions): one good thing, one mistake, one thing to try next.
- **Tilt trend**: correlate mood + streak with performance (deaths, CS) → show the player
  "your deaths go up 40% after 2 losses" using their own data. That is convincing.
- **Toxicity guard**: remind to mute; track "I typed in chat" self-report.
- **Positive tracking**: show improvement in focus metrics even when LP goes down.
- Short TILT-style self-assessment every month (adapted, non-clinical).
